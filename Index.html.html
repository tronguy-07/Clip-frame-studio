How to Run Locally on iPad

Option 1: Run via Localhost Server (Recommended for Safari)

Using a-Shell or iSH on iPad (or your Mac/PC on the same Wi-Fi):

python3 -m http.server 8080

Then open http://localhost:8080 in iPad Safari.

Option 2: Offline Single-File in iPad Files

Tap Save App (.html) in the top bar to save clip-maker.html into your iPad Files app. You can open it anytime locally without internet.

iPad Video Tip: Your videos never leave your iPad. When you export, Safari saves an .mp4 directly to your iPad Downloads/Files—tap Share → Save Video to send it straight to your Camera Roll.
